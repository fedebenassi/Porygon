Pokémon VGC events use the Double Battles format, where players bring six Pokémon and select four Pokémon for battle. All Pokémon’s levels are set to level 50.
Current Regulation Set: I
Paldea Pokédex No. 001–400, Kitakami Pokédex No. 001–200, and Blueberry Pokédex No. 001–242, as well as certain other Pokémon are eligible to participate.
